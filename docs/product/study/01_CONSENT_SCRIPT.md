# 01 — Consent script (DRAFT, unreviewed)

Moderator reads this aloud before any screen is shown. Participant receives the same text in writing and signs/acknowledges. Do not improvise additions.

## Script
"Thank you for taking part. This session tests early versions of a daily practice tool. We are testing the tool, not you. There are no right or wrong answers.

**What happens.** You will try two short prototypes, about eight times each across the session, with a few questions from me between. In total about 60 minutes.

**What it asks.** The prototypes ask you to notice small things, such as what is on your mind or where your attention goes in an afternoon, by choosing from fixed options. Nothing asks you to type personal details. Some prompts may bring up a mild feeling or a thought about your day.

**What it is not.** This is not therapy, a diagnosis, an assessment of you, or medical advice. It will not tell you what is wrong or what to do. It is an early product test.

**You can stop at any time**, for any reason, without explaining and without losing any thank-you we have agreed. You can skip any prompt. Every screen has an exit. If you say stop, I stop.

**What I record.** I take written notes of what you do and say. [No audio or video is recorded. — confirm with reviewer.] Your name is not stored with the notes; you are given a participant code. The prototypes do not save your choices.

**How the notes are used.** To decide what to change in the product. Results are summarised for the Mirar team. Quotes, if used, carry no name. This is a small qualitative test; it does not measure how well the product works.

**If something feels uncomfortable.** Tell me, or just say 'pause'. I will stop the exercise. After the session I will share support contacts: `VERIFY` [contact list from 05_SAFETY_PROTOCOL.md].

**Who to contact afterwards.** `VERIFY` [named contact + email].

Do you have any questions? Do you agree to take part? You can still change your mind later."
