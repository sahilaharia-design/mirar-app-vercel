# 08 — Future design consideration (DOCUMENT ONLY; nothing here is approved or built)

**Principle:** Consistency in value. Variety in experience. Simplicity in interaction.

## Direction
Explore later a varied daily catalog across the existing exercise families, so a person is not served the same format on consecutive days, while the value of the signal stays consistent.

## Protections any such design would need
- **Repetition:** no identical format on consecutive days; sensible gaps per exercise family.
- **Unwanted depth:** variety must not push a person into deeper or heavier exercises than they chose; light by default.
- **Unsupported Mirror inferences:** varied formats must not feed the Mirror conclusions the evidence thresholds do not support; the engine's evidence thresholds and correction rules stay authoritative.
- **Simplicity:** each format stays easy to start unaided (see study codes N1/NS/O).

## Evidence to gather first
Study variety-preference and return-motivation observations (exploratory, 12 people) and the first-exposure comprehension results. They inform whether to proceed; they do not prove demand or retention.

## Explicitly not now
Scheduling, personalization, new catalog mechanics, engine changes, and A9–A11 simulations (held until first human findings). S1a (persist-on-serve) is unrelated and remains unmerged pending independent review.
