# 06 — Participant feedback template (DRAFT)

Read aloud or hand over. Record answers under the participant code. Same questions for both prototypes; ask per prototype.

**Prototype:** ________  **Code:** ______

1. In one sentence, what was this asking you to do?
2. Was anything unclear or awkward? Which screen?
3. Did the options fit your day? (always / mostly / sometimes / rarely)
4. Was there anything you wanted to type or say that the choices didn't allow?
5. How easy was it, 1 (hard) to 5 (very easy)?
6. How much did it feel like something from your own day, 1 (not at all) to 5 (very much)?
7. Did anything feel too personal, pushy or off? (yes / no; if yes, what)
8. Would you do this again tomorrow? (yes / maybe / no) — *this is a stated preference, not a prediction*

**End of session**
9. Of the two, which felt more natural to you, and why? (optional)
10. Anything you'd remove?
11. Anything you'd want in its place?

12. Having tried two different kinds of practice: next time, would you prefer the same kind or a different kind? (same / different / no preference)
13. Why? (verbatim)
14. If you came back, what would bring you back: wanting to see something new, or what the exercise itself gave you? Either, both, or neither is fine. (verbatim; moderator codes RM from the answer)
15. Was there a point where you weren't sure what to do next, or where there was too much on the screen? Which one? (verbatim)

**Gate questions (ask after the FIRST try of each prototype; verbatim for Q1 and Q20)**
16. How interesting or enjoyable was that, 1–5?
17. How heavy or serious did it feel, 1–5?
18. If you had to call it one of these, which: a quiz, a chore, a practice, a game?
19. Would you want to do another one like it? (yes / no)
20. Here is what it said at the end. Is any line untrue about what you did or chose? (yes, which / no)
Also record, without asking leading questions: any discomfort (none / mild / severe) and any feeling of pressure, in the participant's words.

**Close** (say all three, in this order):
1. "The prototypes saved nothing."
2. "I wrote notes under your code, not your name. With your consent I keep them until you ask me to delete them `VERIFY` [retention period]."
3. Hand over support contacts and the deletion contact `VERIFY`.
