# 06 — Participant feedback template (DRAFT)

Read aloud or hand over. Record answers under the participant code. Same questions for both prototypes; ask per prototype.

**Prototype:** ________  **Code:** ______

1. In one sentence, what was this asking you to do?
2. Was anything unclear or awkward? Which screen?
3. Did the options fit your day? (always / mostly / sometimes / rarely)
4. Was there anything you wanted to type or say that the choices didn't allow?
5. How easy was it, 1 (hard) to 5 (very easy)?
6. How much did it feel like something from your own day, 1 (not at all) to 5 (very much)?
7. Did anything feel too personal, pushy or off? (yes / no; if yes, what)
8. Would you do this again tomorrow? (yes / maybe / no) — *this is a stated preference, not a prediction*

**End of session**
9. Of the two, which felt more natural to you, and why? (optional)
10. Anything you'd remove?
11. Anything you'd want in its place?
