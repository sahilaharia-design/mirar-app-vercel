# 05 — Safety protocol (DRAFT; independent review REQUIRED before use)

This is a non-clinical product test of an everyday check-in. The moderator is not a clinician and does not assess anyone.

## 1. Eligibility (screen before booking and re-check at the session)
Include: adults 18+, English-comfortable, not currently in acute distress.
Exclude (decline politely, offer no reason beyond "not a fit for this test"): anyone who says they are currently in crisis, bereaved within the last month, recently hospitalised for mental health, or currently receiving treatment where the clinician advised against such exercises; anyone who is a Mirar customer or close contact of the moderator.

## 2. Content limits
Prototypes use fixed options, no free typing, no probing of past events, no diagnostic wording, and do not tell the person what to do. The moderator does not ask for details of what the participant is dealing with.

## 3. Stop triggers (any one → stop at once)
- Participant says stop, pause, or asks to leave.
- Visible distress: crying, shaking, withdrawal, or saying the content is upsetting.
- Any mention of self-harm, harm to others, abuse, or inability to cope.
- Moderator concern, even without a stated reason.

## 4. On a stop
1. Stop the prototype. Close the screen. Say: "We can stop here. You did nothing wrong."
2. Do not analyse or interpret. Offer a break, water, and to end the session.
3. Offer the support contacts (§5). Do not push; do not promise confidentiality beyond what 01 states.
4. If there is mention of immediate danger: stay with the person, encourage contacting local emergency services or the helpline, and, if they are with you in person and unable to, call emergency services. Escalation owner: `VERIFY` (named person).
5. Thank-you is honoured in full.
6. Log the incident the same day (§6). Notify the founder and the reviewer.

## 5. Support contacts (VERIFY every number and hours before printing)
- National tele-mental-health helpline (India): `VERIFY` (Tele-MANAS 14416 — confirm current number and hours).
- Emergency services: `VERIFY` (112 in India — confirm).
- A local clinician or counselling service chosen by the adviser: `VERIFY`.

## 6. Incident log
`date, participant_code, prototype, try_no, trigger, action taken, outcome, who was informed, follow-up date`. No identifying details beyond the code.

## 7. Study halt rule
Two stop events related to content, or any serious incident, pauses the study until the reviewer and founder decide.

## 8. Moderator preparation
Read this protocol and the consent script together; rehearse the stop script once; keep the contact sheet on the table.
