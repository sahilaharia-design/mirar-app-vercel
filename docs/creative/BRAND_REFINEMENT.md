# Mirar — the mark becomes the system

Creative refinement, 8 October 2026. This supersedes the visual recommendations in the first `CREATIVE_REVIEW.md`; that document remains the record of the initial comparison. Explorations remain separate from the authoritative application.

## What the canonical assets actually change
The mark is **an oblique, softly bounded oval with a textured, luminous surface**. It is not a perfect circle, a camera aperture, an eye, a progress ring or a scientific specimen. The hand-drawn wordmark supplies humanity; the mark supplies an opening with an incomplete, changing reading.

The repository assets were checked against the newly supplied canonical files. `mirar-mark.png` contains exactly the same RGBA artwork pixels as `Only Logo.png`, with transparent outer margins removed; `mirar-wordmark.png` likewise matches `Just Name.png`. The existing asset files are unchanged. No outline was traced, no replacement oval drawn, no new gradient generated, and no logo recoloured or mirrored.

The creative drawings use the **actual PNG alpha as their mask**. There is no hand-approximated elliptical path. The intact source image and an independent reading layer are separate SVG elements. The mask clips the content layer; it does not replace the canonical logo. This is a product window inspired by the mark, not a new logo lockup. The wordmark appears in the same header/footer positions as before; distinction comes from structure, not more placements.

This makes four useful rules possible:
1. **The opening is Mirar’s; the response is the user’s.** Mark-defined edges identify places where something can be noticed, without clipping readable text into an oval.
2. **A response and a reading are separate planes.** They can be placed alongside one another, examined separately and revised. Alignment of UI planes is not a psychological measurement.
3. **Clarity does not mean certainty.** A gap remains visible; no “fully clear” reward, forced reveal or progress percentage.
4. **Looking again is an action.** Story controls reposition reading layers; observation controls expose limits; disagreement uses the approved correction flow.

The luminosity is already in the supplied artwork. It is not permission to add ambient gradients, glass, blurred backgrounds or idle motion elsewhere.

## Direction 01 — Aperture becomes “The revisable window”

### What stays
Editorial confidence; ivory/paper/charcoal; restrained clay accents; Instrument Serif/DM Sans; one clear practice at a time; exact response receipts; supported choice/timeframe/commitment controls; equal feedback and all Partly reasons.

### What changes
The circular lens, reticle, calibration marks and optical-instrument vocabulary are removed. The frame no longer shrinks toward an implied “better focus” state. Its outline is the exact canonical alpha, held stable while **content planes** change relationship. Burnt clay no longer dominates the hero illustration; the source artwork’s own luminous texture returns as the primary brand material.

### How the mark shapes the system
- **Hero/reveal:** the source-defined opening holds two separate reading planes. Their gap follows the mark’s oblique orientation without redrawing its boundary.
- **Today:** a quiet mark-defined edge introduces Notice → Choose → Look again; it is an invitation, not a status indicator.
- **Inner Rep:** paired options retain full rectangular hit areas and unmasked live text. A small mark-defined opening connects the response line to its selected frame. Nothing is draggable-only or precision-dependent.
- **CTA:** a restrained source-defined reveal sits behind the arrow, within the ordinary accessible button.
- **Mirror:** the exact-response and interpretation surfaces have unequal *provenance roles*, not unequal truth status. A source-defined edge identifies the reading boundary; uncertainty remains a separate open region.

### How the Mirror shapes the interactions
“Notice what is yours” → “Look again” → “Leave room to revise” is the authored story. The diagram repositions layers only when requested. The sample’s choice remains exact; it is never rewritten as an insight. The Mirror offers **Read separately / Read alongside**, making it possible to inspect a reading without confusing it with the response. Turning the observation over exposes its limit; Partly opens the unchanged structured correction.

### Updated samples
- **Hero:** “Look within. Leave room to look again.” Supporting line: “A small daily practice. Your own words. A reflection you can always revise.” The art is the Mirar opening with a response plane, a reading plane and a visible boundary.
- **Today / rep:** “One honest moment.” The brand edge and Notice / Choose / Look again instruction lead into the actual “Which is closer to today?” template. Both offered truths and unknown are legitimate; selection is confirmed explicitly.
- **Mirror:** “A reflection. Never the whole picture.” A user response, a fictional engine reflection and uncertainty remain explicitly separate. Reading-layout controls change presentation only, not eligibility or evidence.

### Coherence and distinctiveness
The visitor learns the same relationship on the landing that they later use in the product: enter an opening, name what is yours, examine a separate reading, retain the right to revise. Mirar’s shape and Mirar’s epistemic boundary become one recognisable interaction grammar. It is no longer a generic focus-product instrument carrying a Mirar wordmark.

**Risk:** printing the logo everywhere, squeezing prompts into the silhouette, or treating the gap as a clarity meter would undo the idea. Keep large expression at arrival, small framing inside practice, and plain text where evidence must be read.

## Direction 02 — Weave becomes “What we carry forward”

### What stays
Warm material language; plum ink, oat, dry ochre; horizontal response ribbons; intentional action as something carried into life; no continuous chain to protect.

### What changes
The large freestanding textile grid disappears. Interlacing is now held **inside Mirar’s source-defined opening**, not an unrelated craft world. The few lines stand for a moment, a possible reading and the room between them. They are authored explanatory material, not measured patterns, days or traits.

### How the mark shapes the system
The mark is the stable container; ribbons enter and leave it rather than filling a grid. Its tilt informs the oblique meeting point between response and reading. A narrow material edge ties Today, the response ribbon and the Mirror surfaces together. The wordmark’s human gesture is respected through conversational pacing rather than redrawn handwriting.

### How the Mirror shapes the interactions
A moment belongs to the user. A reading can meet it but cannot replace it. Story states reveal the relationship on request; revising the reading does not remove the original response. **Read separately** untangles the presentation into two surfaces. A commitment remains the user’s exact choice and timeframe, with no guilt, completion colour or overdue framing.

### Updated samples
- **Hero:** “A moment is yours. Its meaning can change.” A few interlacing paths live within the actual Mirar boundary.
- **Today / rep:** the Today edge is quieter than the landing; exact answers become broad, equally weighted ribbons. The selected ribbon is held, not rewarded. Continue emits the original option ID.
- **Mirror:** “A practice with texture.” Exact response and possible reading occupy separate material planes. Their relationship can be loosened by the layout control; no woven “pattern” is asserted from the sample.

### Coherence and distinctiveness
The material now has a Mirar reason to exist: it shows the difference between something experienced and a revisable meaning. It is not a decorative tapestry attached to a wellness product.

**Risk:** texture can still obscure provenance, and growing density can look like progress. Never connect the number of strands, their tightness or their completeness to the user’s frequency or mental state.

## Direction 03 — Fieldnotes becomes “The open reading”

### What stays
Evidence discipline; exact-choice provenance; thoughtful marginalia; the ability to revisit an observation; live, readable text; separation of response, reading and uncertainty.

### What changes
The botanical-like specimen, arbitrary cutout ring and scientific-looking study marks are removed. Paper rotations and notebook theatre are reduced. The actual Mirar opening now frames the reading, while restrained leaders identify **response / reading / what remains open**. This is not a journal or a diagnostic field guide.

### How the mark shapes the system
The source silhouette provides the annotation container and a repeated boundary edge, not a stamp. On mobile, the edge stays narrow and text reflows outside it. The large illustration, small choice frame and Mirror provenance edge use the same source geometry; no alternative brand symbol competes with the canonical mark.

### How the Mirror shapes the interactions
The user can compare the exact offered response with an explicitly labelled engine-generated example, inspect its evidence, qualify it, reject it or remain unsure. **Read separately** privileges careful inspection over an attractive combined story. Turning an observation over reveals “One response isn’t a pattern.” The control never supplies a new psychological interpretation.

### Updated samples
- **Hero:** “What you said. What it might mean. Room between.” The opening holds annotated planes, with no claim to have read the visitor.
- **Today / rep:** “One honest moment.” The question is legible and dominant; choices are response slips, not journal entries. Optional words remain unstored through the unchanged components.
- **Mirror:** “An open notebook. Not a verdict.” This retained heading is deliberately metaphorical; the functional architecture is response / reading / uncertainty. It stores no notebook and creates no archive. A future implementation should test whether “open reading” communicates the product more clearly than notebook copy.

### Coherence and distinctiveness
The landing teaches a distinction the Mirror actually lets the user operate. The mark’s surface becomes a place for partial knowledge rather than a graphic specimen of the person.

**Risk:** this remains the most analytical direction. Too much marginalia could crowd the experience or imply scientific authority. Keep evidence plain, readable and conditional; avoid annotations without real provenance.

## Recommendation: Mirar Aperture / The revisable window
Aperture remains the strongest direction **only after this change**. I would not select the earlier circular lens. The final system should be built around:

- **Actual source geometry:** the canonical mark’s alpha defines the opening. Never substitute a generic oval, circle or approximate path.
- **A stable boundary, revisable content:** the window does not grow with “fitness.” Its reading planes can be separated, examined and corrected.
- **One repeated operation:** look within → respond honestly → look again → live. This ties discovery to Today and the Mirror.
- **Distinct provenance:** what the user said is never visually merged into what Mirar infers. Fieldnotes contributes this discipline, not another palette or specimen vocabulary.
- **Brand expression in service of reading:** generous opening on the landing; quiet edge in the rep; explicit boundary in the Mirror; utility remains ordinary and easy.
- **Source luminosity, not ambient effects:** preserve the canonical artwork, grain and colours. Keep other surfaces warm, tactile and restrained.
- **Uncertainty without failure:** open space is legitimate. Missing evidence does not animate into an answer. Corrections do not crack, stain or dim the window.

Weave is the warmer alternative; Fieldnotes is the clearer evidence tool. Aperture offers the most coherent whole-product verb, now grounded in a shape Mirar already owns and an interaction promise Mirar already makes. This is a design recommendation, not proof of preference or retention.

## Boundaries and implementation
All changes are isolated to the creative review. Real contracts, canonical assets, production components, engine, schema, persistence, safety and evidence rules remain unchanged. Story artwork is authored metaphor; the future Mirror is labelled fictional and still requires the approved read model. No interpretation is generated from a sample response.

The updated preview deployment uses one separate project, `mirar-creative-review`, in the **preview** environment. It is not the app or existing landing project. Its public access applies only to this creative project. The comparison link receives a built, memory-only reference under `/baseline/` so sharing does not lead to localhost. Production domains and the authoritative app remain untouched.
