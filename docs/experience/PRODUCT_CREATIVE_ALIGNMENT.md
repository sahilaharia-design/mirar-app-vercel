## Typed Mirror review: actual behavior versus documentation

Existing suite was rerun from an isolated exported snapshot: **nine checks pass**, 24 simulated users / 430 statements, six directly rejected readings not reproduced. No engine code changed. Focused checks then reproduced:

1. A real reading marked Partly + Something important is missing remains printed unchanged, with a correction suffix. Violates unchanged qualified-reading boundary.
2. A served variant “What's taking up the most room in your head today?” becomes default “What's most on your mind today?” in the receipt.
3. Valid contextual answer “Not today” to “Did work pull at your attention today?” disappears from the ledger.
4. A supplied work topic with no training reps produces “Mirar has not been told anything in these areas.” Absence of trained capacity is not absence of known context.

Source review additionally finds missing matching support records, missing denominator/provenance fields in some facts, unchecked current eligibility for old related claims and untested retention trimming. These findings require a repaired **read model**, not a weakened frozen engine or UI workaround. [Evidence and reproduction](reconciliation-validation/README.md).
