# Mirar — Product × Creative alignment

8 October 2026. Draft based on inspected implementations and the user's brief. **Claude's actual product blueprint is missing; no claim to have reviewed or agreed with it.** The unified experience is not implemented or deployed yet. See [shared contract](MIRAR_UNIFIED_EXPERIENCE_CONTRACT.md) for input/output, support, privacy, empty states and acceptance criteria for every proposed feature.

## Evidence-based convergence

Select **Aperture / The revisable window** as the leading grammar. The existing implementation has demonstrated technical viability across five viewports and meaningful separation controls; it has not demonstrated superior comprehension, retention or efficacy in user research. Weave's palette/textile grid and Fieldnotes' notebook/specimen identity should not be combined with it. Retain only their useful product ideas: carry a user's intention; distinguish provenance and uncertainty.

Keep the source-defined window where it helps explain framing. Reject it when it reduces readability, expands empty space, obscures evidence or delays a task. Text, semantic controls and the user's intention outrank the metaphor. The silhouette stays stable; content is revisable. A brighter/full window never signifies a better person or more fitness.

## Return-state review — not a feature timetable

| Review scenario | Useful distinction | Unacceptable substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 fixture | One useful practice; no finding needed | Forced insight or quiz-derived profile |
| Day 3 fixture | Actual draft/count or user-chosen carrying, if supplied | “We're learning who you are” from elapsed days |
| Day 7 fixture | Supported facts and possibly eligible current reading | Automatic weekly revelation/score |
| Day 30 fixture | Read-model-supported accumulation and explicit gaps | Fabricated arc of growth or filled silhouette |

These labels belong in development controls. Actual UI depends on available facts and eligibility, not elapsed-day thresholds.

## Challenges to resolve with the actual blueprint

1. Define meaningful immediate outcomes without making every rep advice or an action. Exact receipt alone may remain insufficient; ornamental graphics do not fix it.
2. Specify the value of an ordinary day with no eligible reflection. Increasing insight frequency to satisfy layout is unacceptable.
3. Distinguish the response just selected from the evidence that actually supports a reflection. The exploration currently juxtaposes a current sample with unrelated fictional work evidence; explicit labelling prevents a claim, but a real product must use supplied provenance rather than that layout shortcut.
4. Specify what correction acknowledgement can truthfully promise. Submission is observable; future eligibility is policy-driven. Expose only verified events, not a simulated instant “learning” animation.
5. Clarify which integration outcomes and new exercises have real authored/schema support. No rank/drag/compare-event-to-interpretation renderer without appropriate data.
6. Confirm the narrow new remote Mirror facts contract and what is still absent. Do not keep obsolete “all history unavailable” messaging after capacities/carrying exist.
7. Resolve durable-history promises against current device-local retention and sign-out wipe before public product copy is finalized.
