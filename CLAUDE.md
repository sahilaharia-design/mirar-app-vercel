# MIRAR — MOBILE APP PROJECT CONTEXT

## WHAT MIRAR IS
Mirar is a continuous internal alignment system. Think WHOOP — but for your inner state instead of your body. Just like WHOOP never stops reading your biometrics, Mirar never stops reading your internal signals.

It is daily emotional and mental hygiene. Like brushing your teeth — not a 28-day program you complete, but something you do every single day because your internal state shifts every single day.

The core question Mirar exists to answer: "Am I still aligned with who I actually am — or have I been making decisions from an outdated version of myself?"

## WHAT THIS APP IS NOT
- Not a 28-day program or challenge
- Not a journaling app
- Not a meditation or mindfulness app
- Not therapy, coaching, or self-help
- Not a productivity optimizer
- Not something you complete

## WHAT THIS APP IS
- A permanent daily companion that reads your internal state
- A living dashboard that tracks alignment signals continuously over time
- An AI mirror that notices drift before you do
- A system that gets smarter about YOU the longer you use it
- 2-3 minutes per day maximum — as frictionless as brushing teeth

## THE 6 ALIGNMENT THEMES (tracked continuously, not in stages)
1. Inner Alignment & Purpose (IAP)
2. Energy & Well-being (EWB)
3. Focus & Flow (FAF)
4. Relational Capital (RC)
5. Growth & Learning (GAL)
6. Resilience & Action (RA)

## THE BETA (what exists now — do not replicate)
The current beta at mirar.life validated the concept through a structured 28-day email-based program. That was proof of concept only. The real product is continuous, not cyclical. The app graduates Mirar from validation into a permanent daily system.

## WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT FROM EVERYTHING ELSE
- WHOOP reads your body every day → Mirar reads your inner state every day
- Not static like personality tests (Myers-Briggs, Enneagram)
- Not reactive like therapy (you go when something breaks)
- Not dependent on another human like coaching
- The only system that tracks purpose misalignment, emotional drift, and decision disconnect continuously
- Gets more accurate the longer you use it — it learns your patterns

## THE AI MIRROR LAYER
After each daily check-in the AI reflects back a short synthesis — not advice, not coaching, just a mirror. "Here is what your signals showed today." It tracks drift across days and surfaces patterns the user cannot see themselves. If your energy has been dropping for 6 days, Mirar notices before you do.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES
- Soft dark mode, warm undertones
- Mirror / water / light metaphors
- Calm, generous whitespace — nothing feels rushed
- 2-3 minute daily interaction maximum
- No gamification, no streaks for competition, no comparison to others
- Every screen feels like looking inward, not outward
- Poetic but not heavy. Calm but not cold.

## TECH STACK
- React Native with Expo
- Use ui-ux-pro-max skill for all UI/UX decisions

## BUILD ORDER
1. Onboarding (What is Mirar, How the daily check-in works, Privacy promise)
2. Home screen (Today's signal card, 6-theme alignment ring, streak of days, last drift alert)
3. Daily check-in (adaptive 3-5 questions based on recent signals)
4. AI Mirror reflection screen (post check-in synthesis — signal not advice)
5. Alignment Dashboard (6 themes trending over time — weekly, monthly view)
6. Drift Alert system (when a theme drops consistently, surface it gently)
